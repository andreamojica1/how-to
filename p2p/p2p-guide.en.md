# Peer to peer guide

After reading this guide you will know what peer-to-peer fundraising is on Afrus, what it is for, how to change its images, what a supporter goes through after signing up, and how to review, approve and measure your fundraisers' pages.

*Module: Peer to peer · Menu: **Fundraising > Peer to peer fundraisers** · Audience: fundraising and community teams*

> **About the labels in this guide:** buttons, fields and menus are written exactly as they appear in the Spanish version of the console, with an English explanation where it helps.

---

## What Peer to peer is

**Peer to peer lets your supporters create their own fundraising page for one of your organization's campaigns.** We call each supporter who creates a page a fundraiser.

Each page has its own goal, message and donation form. Your organization reviews these pages from the console and decides whether to approve, reject or end them.

## What it is for

- **Multiplying a campaign's reach.** Each supporter shares their personal page with their own community.
- **Seeing who is fundraising in a single list.** You know who created a page, what status it is in and what its goal is.
- **Controlling what gets published.** You approve, reject or end your fundraisers' pages.
- **Attributing each donation to its fundraiser.** The donation is linked to the page it came from, and that page shows how much it has raised against its goal.

## Before you start: turn on Peer to peer in your campaign

Everything else depends on this step.

1. **Open the campaign.** Go to **Fundraising > Campañas**, open the campaign and go to the **Peer to peer** tab.
2. **Turn on the switch.** It is next to the "Peer to peer" title. You will see the message *"Peer to Peer activado"* (Peer to Peer enabled).
3. **Choose the donation widget.** In **Seleccionar widget** (Select widget), choose the widget that will appear on your fundraisers' pages. This way donors on every page give through the form you choose.
4. **Upload both images.** Follow "How to change the cover images". While one is missing you will see **Configuración requerida** (Setup required): *"Para el correcto funcionamiento del módulo peer to peer, asegúrate de subir ambas imágenes."* (For the peer to peer module to work properly, make sure you upload both images.)

When you finish, every donation widget linked to that campaign shows a "Crea tu campaña" (Create your campaign) button below the form. That button takes your supporters to the fundraiser sign-up page. Its text and color (gradient, dark or light) can be customized in the widget settings.

> **Important:** if a widget has several peer to peer campaigns, the button uses the first one.

# Step by step

## How to change the cover images

There are two sets of images: the campaign's, which you set from the console, and each fundraiser's own, which they set in their page editor.

> **Important:** images are locked into a fundraiser's page the moment they click "Publicar" (Publish). If you change the campaign images later, pages already published are not updated, and a fundraiser cannot edit their page once published. So it's best to have the images ready before promoting the campaign.

### Part 1 — Campaign images (you set them)

Peer to peer must be turned on in the campaign: with the switch off, the image buttons are disabled.

1. **Open the Peer to peer tab.** In **Fundraising > Campañas**, open the campaign and go to the **Peer to peer** tab.
   <!-- screenshot pending: how-to-change-the-p2p-cover-images-01 — campaign Peer to peer tab with the image blocks -->
2. **Choose which image to change.** In **Subir foto de portada** (Upload cover photo — the first image in the top carousel of each page) or **Subir foto de campaña** (Upload campaign photo — the secondary image further down the page), click "Subir" (Upload) to choose a new file, or "Editar" (Edit) to re-crop the current image. "Editar" only appears if there is already an image.
3. **Crop the image.** In the cropper you can choose the **Relación de aspecto** (Aspect ratio) — **9:16 (Vertical)**, **1:1 (Cuadrado)** (square), **4:3 (Estándar)** (standard) or **16:9 (Panorámico)** (widescreen) — and use "Rotar a la derecha" (Rotate right).
4. **Save twice.** Click "Guardar" (Save). The first time it switches to the desktop preview (16:9); check it and click "Guardar" again to upload the image.

When you finish you will see *"Foto de portada actualizada"* (Cover photo updated) or *"Foto de campaña actualizada"* (Campaign photo updated). You can check the result with "Vista previa" (Preview).

**Formats and sizes:** jpg, jpeg, png, gif and webp files up to 5 MB are accepted. The screen recommends at least 1440 × 530 pixels. If the file is over 5 MB or is not a valid image, you will see *"Error al actualizar la foto de portada"* (or *"…la foto de campaña"*) — error updating the photo. If a campaign is missing either image, the fundraiser's page shows a generic one.

> **Can it be undone?** You can upload another image at any time, but the change only reaches pages published afterwards.

### Part 2 — Fundraiser images (they set them)

Each fundraiser does this in the editor that opens after signing up:

5. **Change their cover image.** In **Imagen de portada** (Cover image), click "Reemplazar" (Replace). It is the second image in the top carousel. Recommended size: 1080 × 1920 px, under 500 KB.
6. **Change their profile photo.** In **Foto de perfil** (Profile photo), click "Seleccionar archivo" (Choose file). It is the photo shown next to their name. Recommended size: 1080 × 1080 px, under 500 KB.

> **Important:** the "Editar" button on the fundraiser's cover image does nothing for now. To change the image, the fundraiser should use "Reemplazar".

## What happens after a supporter fills in the form

There are two different forms: the sign-up form, filled in by someone who wants to become a fundraiser, and the donation form, filled in by someone donating on a fundraiser's page.

### Part 1 — A supporter signs up as a fundraiser

1. **They fill in the sign-up form.** From the widget's "Crea tu campaña" button they reach the sign-up page and fill in **Nombre \*** (first name), **Apellido \*** (last name), **Correo electrónico \*** (email), **Teléfono \*** (phone), **País \*** (country) and **Objetivo de recaudación (en USD)** (fundraising goal in USD). They tick **Acepto términos y condiciones** (I accept the terms and conditions) and click "INICIAR MI CAMPAÑA SOLIDARIA" (or "CREAR CAMPAÑA") — start my campaign.
2. **Their page is created as Pending.** It appears immediately in your list under **Fundraising > Peer to peer fundraisers** and counts in **Solicitudes pendientes** (Pending requests). If they leave the goal empty, their goal is the campaign's goal. If that email already has a page in that campaign, they see *"Ya tienes un diseño para esta campaña"* (You already have a design for this campaign) with an "Iniciar sesión" (Log in) button.
3. **They design their page.** The editor opens. Their message is pre-filled with AI-generated text, which they can regenerate with "✨ Generar mi historia con IA" (Generate my story with AI); if no AI text is configured, a default message is used. They can upload their images, write their **Mensaje** (message) and add a video in **Agrega un video (Opcional)** (Add a video, optional) → "Insertar" (Insert).
4. **They publish.** When they click "Publicar":
   - their page is generated, with an address like `web.afrus.org/peer-to-peer-campaign<number>`;
   - your organization's contact email receives an email with the subject *"Tu campana ha sido creada de forma exitosa"* (Your campaign has been created successfully), with the campaign name, the fundraiser's name, the date and the page number;
   - the fundraiser sees *"¡Campaña creada exitosamente!"*: *"Tu campaña será aprobada por la organización y te enviarán un correo cuando quede lista para que inicies a empoderar a tu comunidad."* (Your campaign will be approved by the organization, and you will get an email when it is ready.)
5. **They wait for approval.** While the page is Pending, if they try to log in to their dashboard they see *"Tu campaña está en revisión, te notificaremos cuando sea aprobada"* (Your campaign is under review; we'll let you know when it's approved).
6. **They get the answer by email.** When you approve or reject their page, the fundraiser receives *"Tu campaña fue aceptada"* (accepted) or *"Tu campaña fue rechazada"* (rejected) — always in Spanish.
7. **They log in to their dashboard.** Once approved, they log in to their **Mis campañas** (My campaigns) dashboard with their email and phone. They receive a 6-digit verification code by email, which expires after 10 minutes.

> **Important:** if a supporter signs up but never clicks "Publicar", the request still appears in your list as **Pendiente**, but with no page ("Ver página" opens nothing), and your organization does not receive the notification email.

### Part 2 — Someone donates on a fundraiser's page

1. **The donor fills in the widget.** The fundraiser's page includes the donation widget you chose in the **Peer to peer** tab. It works like any donation.
2. **The donation is attributed to the fundraiser.** It is linked to that fundraiser's page and to the campaign.
3. **It adds to the page total.** When the donation succeeds, it adds to the **Total recaudado** (Total raised) shown on the fundraiser's public page.
4. **Thank-you page.** After donating, the donor sees the thank-you or redirect page set up in the widget.

> **Pending confirmation:** whether the fundraiser or your organization receive an email each time someone donates on a fundraiser's page.

## How to see who is doing Peer to peer, approve a fundraiser and know how much they have raised

### Part 1 — See who is fundraising

1. **Open the dashboard.** Go to **Fundraising > Peer to peer fundraisers**.
   <!-- screenshot pending: how-to-manage-p2p-fundraisers-01 — Peer to Peer dashboard with cards and Fundraisings table -->
2. **Read the cards.** **Solicitudes pendientes** counts pages awaiting approval; **Total Campañas**, all pages in the list in any status; **Total Fundraisers**, how many different people have pages.
3. **Check the Fundraisings table.** It is sorted newest first. You can sort it by **ID**, **Campaña** and **Meta**.

| Column | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Three-dot menu | "Ver detalle" (View details) and "Ver página" (View page — opens the public page in a new tab) |
| **ID** | Fundraiser page number; clicking it opens the details |
| **Estado** (Status) | **Activa** (Active), **Rechazada** (Rejected), **Pendiente** (Pending) or **Finalizada** (Ended) |
| **Fecha Inicio** (Start date) | Your campaign's creation date, not the date the fundraiser signed up |
| **Campaña** (Campaign) | Public name of the campaign |
| **Fundraiser** | Fundraiser's first name, last name and email, or *Sin usuario asignado* (No user assigned) |
| **Meta** (Goal) | The page's goal |

4. **Open a fundraiser's details.** In the three-dot menu, choose "Ver detalle" (or click the **ID**). At the top you'll see the page address, with buttons to copy it and to "Ver página". **Información básica** (Basic information) shows the sign-up date (**Fecha creación de la campaña**), **Nombre**, **Apellido**, **Correo electrónico** and **Número de teléfono**. **Origen de contacto** (Contact source) shows **Campaña**, **Widget**, **URL página** and **Detalle de la fuente** (Source detail).

### Part 2 — Approve (or reject) a fundraiser

Every new page starts as **Pendiente** and needs your approval: there is no automatic approval.

5. **Open the page's details.** From the list, three-dot menu → "Ver detalle". Before deciding, you can review the content with "Ver página".
6. **Choose what to do.** The buttons depend on the status:
   - **Pendiente:** "Aprobar campaña" (Approve), "Rechazar campaña" (Reject) and "Finalizar campaña" (End).
   - **Rechazada:** "Aprobar campaña" and "Finalizar campaña".
   - **Activa:** only "Finalizar campaña".
   - **Finalizada:** no buttons.
7. **Approve.** Click "Aprobar campaña". It acts immediately, without confirmation, and you'll see *"Campaña aprobada con éxito"* (Campaign approved successfully). The page becomes **Activa** and the fundraiser receives the email *"Tu campaña fue aceptada"*; from then on they can log in to their dashboard.

   If after approving you see an error instead of the details, go back to the list: the status may already be **Activa** even though the screen shows the error.

8. **Or reject / end.** "Rechazar campaña" and "Finalizar campaña" ask for confirmation: *"Esta acción eliminará la landing de la campaña y no podrá volver a accederse a la URL. ¿Deseas continuar?"* (This will delete the campaign page and its URL will no longer work. Continue?). Rejecting sets the status to **Rechazada** and the fundraiser receives *"Tu campaña fue rechazada"*. Ending sets it to **Finalizada** and no email is sent.

> **Can it be undone?** Approving cannot go back to Pending: an active page can only be ended. Rejecting and ending are permanent for the page: its address stops working. Even if you later click "Aprobar campaña" on a rejected page, the status changes to **Activa** but the page is not published again.

### Part 3 — Know how much each fundraiser has raised

9. **Open the fundraiser's public page.** In the list, three-dot menu → "Ver página". The top block shows **Total recaudado** (Total raised), the goal and the percentage reached.
   - It only adds successful donations to that campaign made from that page; it doesn't count attempts, pending, failed or deleted donations.
   - It is shown in your organization's currency.
   - It is calculated when the page is opened or reloaded: if you have it open, reload it to see the current figure.

> **Important:** for now, the **Total recaudado** block in the console details always shows $0, whether or not there are donations, and the list shows no amounts. To see how much a fundraiser has raised, use "Ver página". The same happens in the fundraiser's dashboard (**Mis campañas**): its cards show 0 and $0, so it's best to tell them to check their own public page.

**Campaign total:** the Peer to peer module has no total that adds up all pages. Donations made on fundraiser pages are campaign donations, so they count in the campaign's overall figures in **Fundraising > Campañas**.

## Fundraiser page statuses

| Status in your console | How the fundraiser sees it | What it means | What you can do |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Pendiente** (Pending) | En espera (Waiting) | Just signed up; needs your approval | Approve, reject or end |
| **Activa** (Active) | Activa | Approved by your organization | End |
| **Rechazada** (Rejected) | Not shown | Rejected; its published page was deleted | End |
| **Finalizada** (Ended) | Finalizada | Closed by your organization; its published page was deleted | Nothing |

## Known limitations

- **Total raised in the console:** each page's details always show $0, and the fundraiser's dashboard shows 0 donations and $0. To see what was raised, open the public page with "Ver página".
- **No search or status filter** in the console list: you can only sort by **ID**, **Campaña** and **Meta**.
- **"$" symbol:** the **Meta** column and the **Total recaudado** block in the console always use "$", whatever your organization's currency.
- **Goal "(en USD)":** the sign-up form asks for the goal in USD, but the public page shows and compares it in your organization's currency.
- **Fecha Inicio** in the list is your campaign's creation date. The fundraiser's actual sign-up date is in the details, under **Fecha creación de la campaña**.
- **Already published pages:** later changes to the campaign's images or widget don't reach them, and the fundraiser cannot edit their page after publishing.
- **Incomplete details:** the **Widget** field usually shows *No proporcionado* (Not provided) and **Detalle de la fuente** opens empty.
- **Error messages:** if cropping an image fails, the message may appear as technical text; if approving, rejecting or ending fails, the message may be in English.
- **Approval and rejection emails** to fundraisers are always sent in Spanish.

## Frequently asked questions

**Do I have to approve every page?**
Yes. Every new page starts as **Pendiente** and there is no automatic approval.

**How do I get the sign-up link to send to my supporters?**
Today it is reached through the "Crea tu campaña" button on the campaign's donation widgets.

> **Pending confirmation:** whether there is another way to get the fundraiser sign-up link from the console.

**Can a fundraiser change their page after publishing it?**
No. Their dashboard has no option to edit a published page.

**Do fundraiser donations count toward my campaign?**
Yes. They are campaign donations and count in its overall figures.

**How long is the code to log in to the fundraiser dashboard valid?**
10 minutes.
