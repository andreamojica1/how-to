<ul>
<li><strong>Ten el archivo de retorno del banco</strong> (CNAB 400) de boletos generados con Afrus.</li>
<li><strong>Ten una campaña activa</strong> donde registrar las donaciones.</li>

</ol>
<p>Por cada línea con datos completos, Afrus registra una donación exitosa por el valor del boleto, en reales (BRL), en la campaña y el widget elegidos.</p>
<h2>Preguntas frecuentes</h2>
<p><strong>¿Cuánto tarda?</strong> Unos minutos, en segundo plano. La pantalla no se refresca sola: vuelve a cargarla.</p>

=== TUTORIAL ===
Idioma: en
Título: How to create an Itaú import
Slug: how-to-create-an-itau-import
Categoría: Follow up
Resumen: Learn the process to upload the Itaú bank's return file to Afrus and record the donations from paid boletos.
Tiempo de lectura: 2 min
Video: no
Imagen de portada: pendiente
Relacionados: what-are-itau-imports, what-are-itau-imports-for, how-boleto-itau-works
--- CUERPO (HTML) ---
<h2>Before you start</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Have the bank's return file</strong> (CNAB 400) for boletos generated with Afrus.</li>
<li><strong>Have an active campaign</strong> to record the donations in.</li>
<li><strong>Check people's data:</strong> each one needs a CEP (postal code), document type and number.</li>
</ul>
<h2>Step by step</h2>
<p>Labels are shown as they appear in the Spanish version of the console.</p>
<ol>
<li><strong>Open Imports.</strong> Go to <strong>Follow up &gt; Importaciones - itaú</strong> and click "Crear importación" (Create import).</li>
<li><strong>Check the type.</strong> In <strong>Tipo de importación</strong> (Import type) leave <strong>Boleto</strong>.</li>
<li><strong>Choose the campaign.</strong> In <strong>Campaña</strong> (Campaign), choose an active campaign (required).</li>
<li><strong>Choose the widget (optional).</strong> In <strong>Formulario / Widget</strong>. If you don't choose one, the default widget is used.</li>
<li><strong>Upload the file.</strong> Click "Subir archivos" (Upload files) or drag it in. Maximum size: 64 MB.</li>
<li><strong>Import.</strong> Click "Importar" (Import).</li>
<li><strong>Confirm it was created.</strong> You will see <strong>Importación en proceso</strong> (Import in progress). Check that the import appears in the list: the dialog can appear even if the upload failed.</li>
<li><strong>Review the result.</strong> In the row menu, "Ver Líneas" (View lines) shows each line's status and <strong>Mensaje de Error</strong>.</li>
</ol>
<p>For each line with complete data, Afrus records a successful donation for the boleto's amount, in Brazilian reais (BRL), in the chosen campaign and widget.</p>
<h2>Frequently asked questions</h2>
<p><strong>How long does it take?</strong> A few minutes, in the background. The screen doesn't refresh by itself: reload it.</p>
=== FIN ===
