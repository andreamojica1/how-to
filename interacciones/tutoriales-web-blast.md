<p><strong>Fallido</strong> quiere decir que <strong>ninguno</strong> de los correos se pudo enviar. Es distinto de <strong>Alerta</strong> (salieron algunos y fallaron otros) y de <strong>Bloqueado</strong> (el envío se detuvo para proteger tu cuenta).</p>
<h2>Paso a paso</h2>
<ol>

</ol>
<h2>Preguntas frecuentes</h2>
<p><strong>¿Y si dice Bloqueado?</strong> AFRUS detuvo el envío porque hubo demasiados rebotes o quejas. No se puede volver a enviar desde la pantalla: escribe a soporte.</p>
<p><strong>¿Cuál es la causa más común de un envío Fallido?</strong> Que la cuenta quede bloqueada por tener demasiados rebotes y haya que asignarle créditos. Para resolverlo, escribe al correo de soporte.</p>

<p><strong>Does everyone in the group receive it?</strong> No. AFRUS leaves out anyone who can't receive emails: people who unsubscribed, whose address doesn't exist or who didn't agree to receive communications.</p>

Slug: how-to-send-a-blast

Resumen: Learn how to send a Blast step by step: choose the group, prepare the email, send yourself a test and send or schedule it.
Tiempo de lectura: 2 min
Video: no

--- CUERPO (HTML) ---
<p>Labels are shown as they appear in the Spanish version of the console.</p>
<h2>Before you start</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Have your recipient group ready:</strong> a saved segment or a tag.</li>
<li><strong>Verify your email domain (recommended),</strong> so your emails go out with your organization's address.</li>
</ul>
<h2>Step by step</h2>
<ol>
<li><strong>Create the send.</strong> Go to <strong>Interacciones &gt; Blast</strong> and click "Crear envío" (Create send).</li>
<li><strong>Give it a name.</strong> In <strong>Nombre</strong>, click "Agregar" (Add) and type the <strong>Nombre del envío</strong> (send name). Only you see it.</li>
<li><strong>Choose the recipients.</strong> In <strong>Destinatario</strong> (Recipient), click "Agregar". In <strong>Enviar a</strong> (Send to), choose your group in <strong>Segmentos</strong>, <strong>Mis segmentos</strong> (my segments) or <strong>Etiquetas</strong> (tags) and click "Aceptar" (Accept). You will see how many people can receive it.</li>
<li><strong>Choose the channel.</strong> Select <strong>Correo</strong> (Email) and click "Continuar" (Continue).</li>
<li><strong>Say who is sending.</strong> In <strong>Remitente</strong> (Sender), fill in the name, the sending address and the reply-to address. Click "Guardar" (Save).</li>
<li><strong>Write the subject</strong> and click "Guardar".</li>
<li><strong>Choose the design.</strong> In <strong>Contenido</strong> (Content), click "Agregar" (Add) and choose a blank design, one of your templates or an email you already sent.</li>
<li><strong>Send yourself a test.</strong> Click "Enviar prueba" (Send test) and check how it looks.</li>
<li><strong>Send or schedule.</strong> Click "Programar" (Schedule) and choose <strong>Enviar ahora</strong> (Send now) or <strong>Programar fecha</strong> (Schedule date).</li>
</ol>
<p>When you finish you will see <em>"¡Envío realizado satisfactoriamente!"</em> (Send completed) or <em>"¡Envío programado con éxito!"</em> (Send scheduled).</p>
<h2>Frequently asked questions</h2>
<p><strong>Can it be undone?</strong> No. An email that has gone out can't be recalled. Check the group and send yourself a test first.</p>
<p><strong>Can I change a scheduled send?</strong> Yes, while it is in <strong>Borrador</strong> (Draft) or <strong>Programado</strong> (Scheduled). To change the date, use "Reprogramar" (Reschedule).</p>

<li><strong>Encontre os envios com problemas.</strong> Na tabela, os envios em amarelo ou vermelho tiveram mais devoluções ou reclamações.</li>

</ol>
