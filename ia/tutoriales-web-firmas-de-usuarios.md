Resumen: Aprende a crear, editar y eliminar la firma de un usuario, y a usarla como remitente en tus autoresponders con IA.

Relacionados: que-son-las-firmas-de-usuarios, para-que-sirven-las-firmas-de-usuarios
--- CUERPO (HTML) ---
<h2>Paso a paso</h2>
<ol>
<li><strong>Abre las firmas.</strong> Ve a <strong>AI &gt; Firmas de usuarios</strong> y busca al usuario.</li>
<li><strong>Abre su firma.</strong> Haz clic en "Crear firma" (o "Editar firma").</li>
<li><strong>Completa los datos.</strong> Nombre que verán tus contactos, correo de envío, <strong>Cargo para firma</strong> y <strong>Reply to</strong> (el correo donde recibes las respuestas). Todos son obligatorios.</li>

<li><strong>Guarda.</strong> Haz clic en "Crear firma" o "Actualizar firma".</li>
</ol>
<p>Después, al configurar un autoresponder con IA, elige al usuario como <strong>Remitente</strong> en <strong>Configuración IA</strong>.</p>
<h2>Preguntas frecuentes</h2>
<p><strong>¿Puedo eliminar una firma?</strong> Sí, con "Eliminar firma". No se puede deshacer: tendrás que crearla de nuevo.</p>

--- CUERPO (HTML) ---
<p><strong>A signature is a user's business card in the emails the AI writes.</strong> It brings together the name your contacts will see, the sending email, the job title, the email where replies arrive and a short description of the person.</p>
<p>You'll find it in <strong>AI &gt; Firmas de usuarios</strong> (User signatures). Labels are shown as they appear in the Spanish version of the console. Each user is marked <strong>Con firma</strong> (with signature) or <strong>Sin firma</strong> (without signature).</p>
<h2>Frequently asked questions</h2>
<p><strong>Can a user have several signatures?</strong> No. Each user has one signature; if you need to change it, edit it.</p>
=== FIN ===

<h2>Step by step</h2>
<ol>
<li><strong>Open signatures.</strong> Go to <strong>AI &gt; Firmas de usuarios</strong> and find the user.</li>
<li><strong>Open their signature.</strong> Click "Crear firma" (Create signature) or "Editar firma" (Edit signature).</li>
<li><strong>Fill in the details.</strong> Name your contacts will see, sending email, <strong>Cargo para firma</strong> (job title) and <strong>Reply to</strong> (where you receive replies). All are required.</li>

</ol>

<h2>Frequently asked questions</h2>
<p><strong>Can I delete a signature?</strong> Yes, with "Eliminar firma" (Delete signature). It can't be undone: you'll need to create it again.</p>
