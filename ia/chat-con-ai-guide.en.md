# AI Chat guide (Chat con AI)

With this guide you will be able to use AFRUS's AI assistant to ask for ideas, texts and answers about your organization.

*Module: AI · Menu: **AI > Chat con AI** · Audience: the whole organization team*

> **About the labels in this guide:** buttons, fields and menus are written as they appear in the Spanish version of the console, with an English explanation where it helps.

---

## What AI Chat is

**It is an artificial intelligence assistant, chat style, for your organization's team.** You write a question or a request and it answers.

It answers with the information your organization uploaded in **AI > Entrenamiento [AI RAG]**: your documents, your web pages and your instructions.

<!-- screenshot pending: chat-con-ai-01 — chat screen with "Bienvenido a tu asistente de afrus" and the history on the left -->

## What it is for

- **Getting fundraising ideas.** For example, a campaign plan.
- **Writing texts faster.** Donation emails, landing page texts or video scripts.
- **Asking about your documents.** For example: "What did we achieve last year according to the impact report?".

## Before you start

- **Turn on AFRUS AI** from the **Business profile** (profile menu): create your profile and click "Enviar AI" (Send to AI). If you haven't, you'll see the notice *"¡Ups! Parece que no tienes un Business Profile"* (Oops! It looks like you don't have a Business Profile).
- **Upload information in AI > Entrenamiento [AI RAG].** The more you tell the AI, the better its answers. From the chat you get there with the "Configurar" (Set up) button.

# Step by step

## How to use AI Chat

1. **Open the chat.** Go to **AI > Chat con AI**. To start a new conversation, click "Nuevo chat" (New chat).

2. **Write your message.** Type in the box and press Enter to send it. To go to the next line without sending, use Alt + Enter.
   <!-- screenshot pending: chat-con-ai-02 — message box with the model selector and the Interacción avanzada button -->

3. **Wait for the answer.** It can take between 30 seconds and 2 minutes.

4. **Use the answer.** On each answer you can copy it or ask for another one. If you want to change something in your question, edit your message and send it again.
   <!-- screenshot pending: chat-con-ai-04 — assistant answer with the copy and regenerate buttons -->

5. **Pick up a conversation.** Your conversations are saved on the left, in **Historial de conversaciones** (Conversation history).

**Extra options:** next to the message box you can choose the AI model and turn on "Interacción avanzada" (Advanced interaction).

> **Pending confirmation:** what exactly "Interacción avanzada" changes.

**Tip:** be specific. "Write a 150-word email asking for donations for our scholarship campaign, in a warm tone" works better than "write an email".

## What restrictions it has

- **It only knows what you taught it.** It uses your documents, web pages and instructions from **Entrenamiento [AI RAG]**. It doesn't look at your donors', donations' or campaigns' data.
- **You can't attach files in the chat.** If you want the AI to know a document, upload it in **Entrenamiento [AI RAG]**.
- **Each user only sees their own conversations.** The history isn't shared with the rest of the team.
- **Conversations can't be deleted or renamed.**
- **Answers can take up to 2 minutes.** After that, you'll see *"Lo siento, hubo un error al obtener la respuesta."* (Sorry, there was an error getting the answer.) Send your message again.
- **It searches all topics.** Today you can't pick a specific topic or use conversation shortcuts.
- **Always review what the AI writes** before publishing or sending it.

## Known limitations

- When you don't have conversations yet, the sidebar may show technical text instead of "No hay conversaciones" (No conversations). It doesn't affect the chat.

## Frequently asked questions

**Does the AI know my donors?**
No. It only uses what you uploaded in **Entrenamiento [AI RAG]**.

**Can my colleagues see my conversations?**
No. Each user sees their own.
