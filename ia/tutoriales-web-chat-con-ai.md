```text
=== TUTORIAL ===
Idioma: en
Título: How to use AI Chat
Slug: how-to-use-ai-chat
Categoría: AI
Resumen: Learn how to talk with AFRUS's AI assistant, use its answers and pick up your conversations.
Tiempo de lectura: 1 min
Video: no
Imagen de portada: pendiente
Relacionados: what-is-ai-chat, what-is-ai-chat-for, what-restrictions-does-ai-chat-have
--- CUERPO (HTML) ---
<p><em>Labels are shown as they appear in the Spanish version of the console.</em></p>
<h2>Before you start</h2>
<ul><li><strong>Turn on AFRUS AI.</strong> In the <strong>Business profile</strong> (profile menu), create your profile and click "Enviar AI" (Send to AI).</li>
<li><strong>Upload information in AI &gt; Entrenamiento [AI RAG].</strong> The more you tell it, the better its answers.</li></ul>
<h2>Step by step</h2>
<ol>
<li><strong>Open the chat.</strong> Go to <strong>AI &gt; Chat con AI</strong>. For a new conversation, click "Nuevo chat" (New chat).</li>
<li><strong>Write your message.</strong> Press Enter to send it (Alt + Enter for a new line).</li>
<li><strong>Wait for the answer.</strong> It can take between 30 seconds and 2 minutes.</li>
<li><strong>Use the answer.</strong> Copy it or ask for another. You can also edit your message and send it again.</li>
<li><strong>Pick up conversations.</strong> They're saved in <strong>Historial de conversaciones</strong> (Conversation history).</li>
</ol>
<p><strong>Tip:</strong> be specific. Say the type of text, length, tone and topic.</p>
=== FIN ===
```
