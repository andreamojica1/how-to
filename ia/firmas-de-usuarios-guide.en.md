# User signatures guide

With this guide you can create a signature for each person on your team, so the emails the AI writes go out with their name, their email and their way of introducing themselves.

*Module: AI · Menu: **AI > Firmas de usuarios** (User signatures) · Audience: communications and fundraising teams*

> **About the labels in this guide:** buttons, fields and menus are written exactly as they appear in the Spanish version of the console, with an English explanation where it helps.

---

## What it is

**A signature is a user's business card in the emails the AI writes.** It brings together:

- the name your contacts will see;
- the email address the messages are sent from;
- the job title;
- the email address where replies arrive;
- a short description of the person, which you can write yourself or ask the AI to write.

Each user can have only one signature.

## What it is for

- **Choosing who sends the automatic AI emails.** In AI autoresponders, you can only choose users with a signature as the sender.
- **Sending from the right address.** The message goes out from the email you set in the signature.
- **Letting the AI write in that person's voice.** The AI uses the user description when writing.

# Step by step

## How to use it

### Create or edit a signature

1. **Open signatures.** Go to **AI > Firmas de usuarios**. You will see your organization's users and, next to each one, whether they are **Con firma** (with signature) or **Sin firma** (without signature). You can look someone up with "Buscar por nombre" (Search by name).
   <!-- screenshot pending: firmas-de-usuarios-01 — Personalizar usuarios screen with the list, the search box and the Con firma / Sin firma labels -->

2. **Open the user's signature.** Click "Crear firma" (Create signature), or "Editar firma" (Edit signature) if they already have one.

3. **Fill in the basic details.** All are required:
   - **Nombre que verán tus contactos en los correos o mensajes** (name your contacts will see).
   - **Correo desde el que se enviarán los mensajes** (sending email): choose one of your organization's sending addresses.
   - **Cargo para firma** (job title).
   - **Reply to**: the email where you want to receive replies.
   <!-- screenshot pending: firmas-de-usuarios-03 — signature form with name, sending email, job title and Reply to -->

4. **Write the description, or let the AI write it.** The **Descripción de usuario** (user description) is also required, even though it has no asterisk. To have the AI write it:
   - choose the **Tono de comunicación** (tone), for example Formal, Cercano (friendly) or Inspirador (inspiring);
   - if you like, choose an **Estilo de descripción (Opcional)** (style);
   - choose the **Longitud de descripción** (length): Corta, Mediana or Larga (short, medium or long);
   - click "Generar" (Generate).

   If you already have a text, "Mejorar" (Improve) rewrites it and "Regenerar" (Regenerate) creates a new version. You can also write it yourself.
   <!-- screenshot pending: firmas-de-usuarios-04 — Descripción de usuario section with tone, style, length and the Generar, Mejorar and Regenerar buttons -->

5. **Save.** Click "Crear firma" (or "Actualizar firma", Update signature). You will see *"Firma creada exitosamente"* or *"Firma actualizada exitosamente"* (signature created / updated successfully).

The save button only becomes active when the name, email, job title, Reply to and description are all filled in.

### Use the signature

When you set up an AI autoresponder, choose the **Remitente** (Sender) in **Configuración IA** (AI settings). Only users with a signature appear there.

### Delete a signature

1. **Open the user's signature.**
2. **Delete it.** Click "Eliminar firma" (Delete signature) and confirm with "Eliminar".

> **Can it be undone?** No. If you delete a signature, you will need to create it again.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can a user have several signatures?**
No. Each user has one signature; if you need to change it, edit it.
