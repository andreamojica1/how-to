# AI gallery guide

With this guide you can keep your organization's images in one place, to use them in your AI emails, landings, email and WhatsApp blasts, autoresponders and workflows.

*Module: AI · Menu: **AI > Galería para AI** (AI gallery; also in the profile menu as **AI Media**) · Audience: communications and design teams*

> **About the labels in this guide:** buttons, fields and menus are written exactly as they appear in the Spanish version of the console, with an English explanation where it helps.

---

## What it is

**The AI gallery is your organization's image bank.** There you upload, organize and keep the photos you want the AI to use. On screen it is called **IA Media**.

You can add images in three ways: uploading them from your computer, finding them in an image bank, or asking the AI to create one.

## What it is for

- **Putting your photos in the emails the AI writes.** AI emails that use templates (in AI Blast and AI autoresponders) take their main image from here.
- **Choosing which kind of image goes in each email.** With attributes (labels) you tell the AI which images to use. AFRUS picks one at random among those with the right attribute; if none has it, it uses any image in the gallery.
- **Having your images at hand elsewhere.** They also appear in the **IA Media** tab when you choose an image in the template editor and in WhatsApp Business.

# Step by step

## How to use it

### Add images

1. **Open the gallery.** Go to **AI > Galería para AI**.
   ![IA Media screen with the search box, the cargar button, the filters and the images](img/galeria-para-ai-01.webp)

2. **Open the upload window.** Click "cargar" (upload). The **Subir recursos** (Upload resources) window opens.

3. **Choose how to add it.**
   - **Subir recursos** (Upload): first pick the attributes in "Seleccionar atributos" (Select attributes), then click "Subir" (Upload) and choose the file. It uploads as soon as you choose it.
   - **Banco de Imágenes** (Image bank): type what you are looking for (for example, "volunteers"), click "Buscar" (Search) and then click the photo you like.
   - **Generar Imagen** (Generate image): describe the image you want (or use one of the suggested ideas), click "Generar" (Generate) and then click the image to save it.
   ![Subir recursos window with the Subir recursos, Banco de Imágenes and Generar Imagen tabs](img/galeria-para-ai-03.webp)

When you finish you will see *"¡Archivo subido con éxito!"* or *"¡Imagen subida con éxito!"* (file / image uploaded successfully).

**Accepted formats:** JPG, PNG, GIF and WEBP, up to 5 MB.

### Add attributes to an image

1. **Find the image** in the gallery.
2. **Choose its attributes.** On the image card, mark one or more. If you need a new one, click "+", type the **Nombre del atributo** (attribute name) and click "Crear atributo" (Create attribute).
   ![Image cards with the attribute selector and the + button](img/galeria-para-ai-05.webp)

This way the AI knows which images to use in each kind of email.

### Download, copy or delete

- On each image you can "Descargar" (Download), "Copiar enlace" (Copy link) or "Borrar" (Delete).
- To do it with several at once, select them and use the **Acciones** (Actions) menu.
- The screen shows 10 images; to see more, click "Cargar más" (Load more).

> **Can it be undone?** No. A deleted image cannot be recovered.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is there a limit on the number of images?**
No, there is no limit on the number of images.

**Where are these images used?**
In many places, for example in landings, blasts (both email and WhatsApp), autoresponders and workflows.
