# AI Training guide (Entrenamiento [AI RAG])

With this guide you will be able to teach Afrus AI about your organization: upload documents, add web pages and write instructions for it.

*Module: AI · Menu: **AI > Entrenamiento [AI RAG]** · Audience: communications and fundraising teams*

> **About the labels in this guide:** buttons, fields and menus are written as they appear in the Spanish version of the console, with an English explanation where it helps.

---

## What AI Training is

**It is where you give Afrus AI your organization's information.** Here you upload documents, add web pages and write instructions.

The AI uses all of it to answer in **Chat con AI** (AI chat) and in the **Agentes para la web** (the chat you can put on your website). The better the information you give it, the better its answers.

The screen has three tabs: **Documentos** (Documents), **Instrucciones** (Instructions) and **URL'S**.

<!-- screenshot pending: entrenamiento-ai-01 — Training screen with the Documentos, Instrucciones and URL'S tabs -->

## What it is for

- **Making the AI know your organization.** Your cause, your impact, your campaigns and your institutional details.
- **Getting answers based on your information, not generic data.** The chat and your website agent look first at what you uploaded here.
- **Giving it guidelines on tone and content.** You can tell it how to talk about each topic.

## Before you start

- **Turn on Afrus AI.** It is turned on from the **Business profile** (profile menu, top corner): create your profile and, in the last step, click "Enviar AI" (Send to AI). If you haven't, you will see the notice *"¡Ups! Parece que no tienes un Business Profile"* (Oops! It looks like you don't have a Business Profile) with the "Crear Business Profile" (Create Business Profile) button.

# Step by step

## What you upload in Documents and which file types are accepted

In **Documentos** you upload the files that describe your organization: impact reports, institutional presentations, FAQs, descriptions of your programs or campaigns.

**Accepted file types:** PDF, DOC, DOCX, CSV, TXT and PPTX. The screen shows a maximum of 10 MB per file.

> **Pending confirmation:** whether the 10 MB limit is always applied.

1. **Open the Documents tab.** Go to **AI > Entrenamiento [AI RAG]** and choose **Documentos**.
   <!-- screenshot pending: entrenamiento-ai-02 — Documentos tab with the "Suelta aquí tus documentos" drop area -->

2. **Choose your files.** Drag them onto **Suelta aquí tus documentos** (Drop your documents here) or click "Nuevo" (New). You can choose several at once.

3. **Classify them (recommended).** In the **Archivos cargados con éxito** (Files uploaded successfully) window, choose one or more **Atributos** (the document's topic) for each file and type a **Etiqueta** (label; type it and press Enter). If you're not sure, you can do it later.
   <!-- screenshot pending: entrenamiento-ai-03 — Archivos cargados con éxito window with attributes and labels -->

4. **Upload them.** Keep the files you want to upload checked (or use "Seleccionar todos", Select all) and click "Listo" (Done).

When you finish you will see *"Documento guardado exitosamente"* (Document saved successfully) and your files will appear in **Documentos guardados** (Saved documents). The AI reads each document and makes a summary. Once the upload finishes, it can use it.

**Keep in mind:**
- If you don't choose a topic, the document is used for **all** topics.
- You can't upload two files with the same name.
- If a document takes more than 5 minutes to process, the upload fails.

## What you upload in Instructions

In **Instrucciones** you tell the AI, in your own words, what it should know and how it should talk. The screen explains it like this: *"Aquí describe el conocimiento de la AI. Escribe información clave, contexto, tono, y temas principales que debes manejar."* (Describe the AI's knowledge here: key information, context, tone and main topics.)

For example: "We are a foundation that protects forests in Colombia. Speak in a warm, optimistic way. Don't promise figures that aren't in our reports."

1. **Open the Instructions tab.** In **AI > Entrenamiento [AI RAG]**, choose **Instrucciones**.
   <!-- screenshot pending: entrenamiento-ai-04 — Instrucciones tab with one panel per attribute -->

2. **Choose the topic.** There is one panel per attribute (topic). Open the one you want to fill in.

3. **Write your instructions.** Type them in **Escribe aquí tus instrucciones...** (Write your instructions here).

4. **Save.** Click that topic's "Guardar instrucciones" (Save instructions) button. You will see *"¡Instrucciones guardadas con éxito!"* (Instructions saved successfully!)

Instructions apply to your whole organization, and the AI uses them together with that topic's documents.

> **Keep in mind:** topics (attributes) are defined by Afrus; you can't create new ones here.

**Conversation shortcuts:** on this same tab you'll see **Atajos de conversación** (Conversation shortcuts), to save common questions as chat buttons. Today those buttons don't appear in **Chat con AI**, so for now you don't need to set them up.

## What you upload in URLs

In **URL'S** you add your organization's web pages for the AI to read: for example your "About us" page, your programs page or a campaign page.

1. **Open the URL'S tab.** In **AI > Entrenamiento [AI RAG]**, choose **URL'S** (section **Fuentes Web**, Web sources).
   <!-- screenshot pending: entrenamiento-ai-05 — URL'S tab with the Agregar más URL button -->

2. **Add an address.** Click "Agregar más URL" (Add more URLs) and click the new row to open it.

3. **Fill in the details.** Type the address in **URL:** (required). If you want, choose **Atributos** and type labels in **Escribe tu Tag** (Write your tag).

4. **Save.** Click "Guardar cambios" (Save changes). You will see *"¡URL guardada con éxito!"* (URL saved successfully!)

> **Pending confirmation:** whether the AI reads only that page or also the pages linked under it. For now, add your most important pages one by one.

**Keep in mind:** if you click "Guardar cambios" without changing anything, a notice with technical text may appear. Nothing is wrong: your information was already saved.

## How to see what you uploaded

- **Documents:** on the **Documentos** tab, the **Documentos guardados** list shows **Nombre del archivo** (file name), **Tipo** (type), **Atributo**, **Tags** and **Fecha de carga** (upload date). The eye icon downloads the file so you can check it.
  <!-- screenshot pending: entrenamiento-ai-06 — Documentos guardados list with its columns and icons -->
- **Instructions:** on the **Instrucciones** tab, open each topic's panel to read what you wrote.
- **Web pages:** on the **URL'S** tab you see each saved address.

**To delete something,** use the trash icon on its row.

> **Can it be undone?** No. Clicking a document's trash icon deletes it immediately, without asking for confirmation. If you make a mistake, you'll need to upload it again.

> **Keep in mind:** the "Buscar" (Search) button in **Documentos guardados** doesn't filter the list today. To find a document, look through the list by name or date.

## Known limitations

- The **Documentos guardados** search doesn't filter.
- Conversation shortcuts don't appear in the chat.
- Some errors when uploading or deleting documents appear as technical text. If you see one, contact us.

## Frequently asked questions

**How long until the AI uses a new document?**
It can use it as soon as the upload finishes.

**What if I upload a document without choosing a topic?**
It is used for all topics.

**Do labels change how the AI answers?**
> **Pending confirmation:** they are saved today, but it isn't confirmed that they change the answers.
